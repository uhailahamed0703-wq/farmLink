Platform System Architecture & UI/UX Audit

FARMLINK

"Connecting Farms. Growing Businesses."

Farmlink is a production-grade agricultural B2B digital marketplace connecting local verified farmers with commercial food businesses—including restaurants, supermarkets, hotels, and institutional caterers. This document details each screen, interaction pattern, and business functionality across the ecosystem.

Operating Environment Local Development Stack (Node.js / Python HTTP 3000)
Design System Apple iOS Theme • Inter Typography • Emerald & Forest Green
Geographic Pilot Area West Java Agricultural Hub (Lembang, Pangalengan, Cianjur, Sukabumi, Subang)
User Stakeholders Farmers (Producers) • Commercial Buyers (F&B) • Platform Administrators

1. Authentication & Role Selection (`auth.html`)

Security & Access
🔎 Overview & Interaction Architecture
Target Users: Farmers, Commercial Business Buyers, Platform Administrators Endpoint: auth.html

This is the primary gateway for all Farmlink platform users. It provides streamlined authentication with dedicated tabbed registration workflows tailored to specific user types.

  • 1-Click Instant Demo Profiles (Chef Dewa for Buyers, Pak Asep for Farmers, and Farmlink Operations Lead for Admin)
  • Farmer Registration Form with specialized fields: Farm Commercial Name, West Java Location, Land Size (Hectares), and Cultivation Method
  • Business Buyer Registration Form for Restaurants, Supermarkets, Hotels, and Industrial Catering with business addresses and license info
  • Local storage session persistence allowing immediate redirect to role-specific dashboards
Strategic Business Value: Eliminates friction in onboarding rural farmers and busy executive chefs, with clear role separation and rapid demo switching for stakeholders.
