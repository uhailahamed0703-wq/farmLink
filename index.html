How Farmlink Works

A transparent farm-to-door ecosystem engineered specifically for commercial food businesses.

STEP 01

Explore & Filter Harvests

Search real available stock by harvest date, organic certification, and farm location in West Java. Lock in wholesale tier prices.

STEP 02

Schedule or Subscribe

Choose your exact kitchen delivery window (05:00 - 08:00 AM) or automate weekly deliveries for staples like tomatoes and greens.

STEP 03

Refrigerated Delivery & QR Scan

Direct cold-chain truck delivery with QR batch code. Scan to verify harvest time, pesticide lab results, and farm origin.

STEP 01

List Upcoming Harvest

Input expected yield (kg/ton), your farm gate price, harvest date, and upload real photos. No technical complexity required.

STEP 02

Accept Confirmed POs

Receive direct orders from reputable restaurants and supermarkets. Harvest only what is pre-sold at guaranteed fair prices.

STEP 03

Direct Payouts in 24 Hours

Farmlink couriers pick up directly from your farm gate. Payment is deposited directly to your bank account with zero fee deductions.
